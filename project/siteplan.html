Site Plan

This is the site plan for the WDD 231 course.

Accra Chamber of Commerce

The Accra Chamber of Commerce is dedicated to promoting and supporting businesses in the Accra region.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide a platform for businesses in the Accra region to connect with potential customers and partners.

Target Audience

Businesses and professionals looking for networking opportunities and support services in the Accra region.

Scenario

Where can I find reliable business partners in the Accra region?

How can I promote my business to a wider audience in Accra?

Color Scheme

The color scheme for this site will be a combination of #006b3f and white, representing professionalism and cleanliness.

Typography

The typography for this site will be a clean, modern font that is easy to read and provides good contrast against the background.

font-family: Google Sans - To be used for headings

font-family: Times New Roman - To be used for body text

Wireframe

The wireframe for this site will include a header with the site name and navigation, a main content area with the site's primary information, and a footer with copyright information.
